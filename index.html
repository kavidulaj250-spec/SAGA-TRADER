SAGA Trading Calculator

Calculate risk amount, position size, margin and risk/reward across Forex, Gold, Crypto, Indices and Stocks.

Documented assumptions: Forex 1 lot = 100,000 units · Gold 1 lot = 100 oz · Crypto 1 lot = 1 BTC · Indices 1 lot = 10 contracts ($10/point) · Stocks 1 lot = 100 shares. Position size = Risk ÷ (stop distance × contract size). Leverage changes required margin only — never your predefined risk amount.
